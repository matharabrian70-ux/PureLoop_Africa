Privacy Notice.

We respect your privacy. This notice explains what personal information may be handled when you browse this Website or contact PureLoop Africa.

Effective date: 14 November 2026Kenya

This Privacy Notice is intended to be transparent about the limited ways personal information may be handled through this corporate information Website and through enquiries sent to the company. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

1. Who is responsible for your information

For enquiries made through the contact details on this Website, the organisation responsible for handling the information is PureLoop Africa Limited (“PureLoop”, “we”, “us” or “our”), based in Nairobi, Kenya.

Privacy enquiries can be sent to purelooplimited@gmail.com.

2. Scope and current Website features

This Website is a static corporate information site. As at the effective date above, it does not provide visitor accounts, online checkout, an on-site enquiry form, an AI chat assistant or an on-site analytics/advertising script. Visitors can contact us using the published email, telephone or WhatsApp links.

Website features may change. This notice will be updated when a change materially affects how personal information is handled.

3. Information we may handle

The information handled depends on how you interact with us:

  • Information you choose to send: for example, your name, email address, telephone number, organisation, site or project location, the service you are asking about, and the contents of your message or attachments.
  • Communications and business records: information contained in follow-up emails, calls, WhatsApp messages, proposals or service-related correspondence.
  • Basic technical information: when a browser requests a webpage, the website host and technical service providers may process information such as an IP address, browser/device details, requested page and request time as part of delivering, maintaining and securing the service.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry and you have a lawful reason to share it.

4. Why we use information

Depending on the circumstances, we may use relevant information to:

  • respond to a question, enquiry, request for a quotation or service proposal;
  • understand a waste-management or environmental-service requirement and assess whether we can assist;
  • communicate with customers, suppliers, partners and other business contacts;
  • manage and document a business relationship or provide agreed services;
  • meet legal, regulatory, safety, accounting or record-keeping obligations; and
  • maintain the security, availability and integrity of the Website and our communications.

5. Legal grounds for processing

Where required by the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable regulations, we handle personal information on an appropriate lawful basis. Depending on the purpose and context, this may include taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performing a contract, complying with a legal obligation, pursuing a legitimate business interest where your rights do not override that interest, or your consent where consent is required.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal or processing carried out on another valid legal basis.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We may share information where reasonably necessary with personnel who need it to handle an enquiry or provide a service, professional advisers, relevant service providers, or public authorities where required or permitted by law.

The Website is hosted using GitHub Pages. It also loads typefaces from Google Fonts. If you follow a WhatsApp or Instagram link, you leave this Website and interact with the relevant provider. Those providers may process technical or account information under their own privacy notices and terms. We do not control their independent processing.

7. Transfers and safeguards

Some technology or communications providers may process information in countries outside Kenya. Where PureLoop transfers personal data outside Kenya, we will do so only as permitted by the Data Protection Act, 2019 and applicable regulations, including any required safeguards or other valid transfer condition.

8. How long information is kept

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to respond to an enquiry, manage a business relationship, meet applicable legal or record-keeping obligations, resolve a dispute or establish/defend a legal claim. The appropriate period depends on the type of information and the circumstances. Information may then be securely deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of where appropriate.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No website, email, telephone or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use care when deciding what information to send through an online channel.

10. Your data-protection rights

Subject to the conditions and limits in applicable law, you may have the right to be informed about the use of your personal data, request access to personal data held about you, object to all or part of its processing, request correction of false or misleading data, and request deletion of false or misleading data. Other rights or remedies may apply in particular circumstances under Kenyan law.

To make a request, email purelooplimited@gmail.com and describe what you are requesting. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the scope of the request before responding. You may also raise a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

11. Children

This Website is a corporate information resource and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us through this Website or our published contact channels.

12. External websites and links

Links to external websites, social networks and messaging services are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices, not this one. We encourage you to review those notices before sharing information with them.

13. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice to reflect changes to the Website, our operations or legal requirements. The latest version will appear on this page with its effective date. Please review it periodically.
