way to deal with waste.

PureLoop Africa Limited is an environmental management company committed to delivering sustainable, practical and innovative waste management solutions across Kenya.

We work with businesses, institutions, industries, communities, and National and County Governments to reduce environmental impact while recovering value from materials that would otherwise be wasted.
